Tony explores the idea of the “living script.”
Working across visual language, interactive media, and worldbuilding, he uses languages, games, and artifacts to explore ways of engaging with symbols through touch, play, and immersion.

Rooted in early Chinese cosmologies, artistic traditions, and synaesthetic experience, his practice searches for a form of writing that is rule-bound yet fluid, human-made yet alive.


桐是一名「活文字」的探索者。
他的创作横跨视觉语言、互动媒介与世界构筑，通过语言、游戏与器物，探索人与符号之间可触、可玩、可进入的关系。

他的实践植根于中国古代宇宙观、艺术传统与通感经验，探寻一种有规则却流动、由人创造却自有生机的文字。
